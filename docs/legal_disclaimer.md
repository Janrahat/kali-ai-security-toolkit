## What is Illegal?

### Unauthorized Activities Include:

- **Port scanning** networks you don't own/control
- **Exploiting vulnerabilities** without permission
- **Network sniffing** on networks you don't manage
- **Denial of Service** attacks (even testing)
- **Unauthorized access** to any system
- **Data exfiltration** without permission
- **Credential testing** (brute force, etc.) without authorization
- **Social engineering** without consent
- **Web scraping** beyond robots.txt/ToS
- **Packet injection** on unauthorized networks

### Even on "Your" Devices:

- Work laptop → Employer owns it (need permission)
- School computer → School owns it (need permission)
- Shared network → May need other users' permission
- ISP network → You don't own the infrastructure

## Ethical Guidelines

### Responsible Security Research

1. **Get Written Permission First**
   - Scope document defining targets
   - Start/end dates
   - Authorized activities
   - Contact information
   - Legal protections

2. **Follow Scope Exactly**
   - Don't test out-of-scope systems
   - Stay within authorized timeframes
   - Use only approved techniques
   - Stop if you find critical issues

3. **Document Everything**
   - Keep logs of all activities
   - Screenshot authorization
   - Record findings professionally
   - Maintain chain of custody

4. **Report Responsibly**
   - Notify system owners immediately of critical issues
   - Follow coordinated disclosure timelines
   - Don't publicize before patching
   - Respect bug bounty rules

5. **Minimize Impact**
   - Use non-destructive testing
   - Avoid disrupting services
   - Don't access/modify user data
   - Clean up after testing

## Specific Tool Warnings

### AI Port Scanner
- Generates network traffic that may trigger IDS/IPS
- Can be detected and blocked by firewalls
- May violate ToS of cloud providers/ISPs
- Some countries ban port scanning entirely

### Reconnaissance Framework
- DNS queries may be logged by target
- Subdomain enumeration may violate APIs' ToS
- Web crawling must respect robots.txt
- May trigger rate limiting/blocking

### Exploit Suggester
- Downloading exploits may be logged
- Using exploits without permission is illegal
- Some exploits are malware themselves
- Research only - not for execution

### Traffic Analyzer
- Packet capture of others' traffic is illegal
- Must have network owner permission
- Violates privacy laws in many jurisdictions
- Workplace monitoring requires HR approval
